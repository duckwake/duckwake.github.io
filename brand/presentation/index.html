What we heard

The brief

An open-source streaming layer for DuckLake: write a view in SQL and DuckWake keeps a lake table up to date from Kafka, exactly once, without a streaming cluster. For data engineers already on DuckDB and DuckLake.

preciselightcandid

Success: a duck people recognise at once, the wake carried by the duck itself, distinct from DuckDB's yellow duck head and from the wave curls of streaming brands, legible at 16 px, works in one colour.

Concept A

Carved, trailing wake (B3)

A duck whose own wake is carved through its body and runs on behind it as streaks.

  • One silhouette carries both ideas: the duck and the wake it leaves
  • Built from one circle family and 45° lines; wake slits are real holes, so it works in one colour
  • A small-size cut (one wide slit, larger eye) keeps it legible at 16–32 px
  • Paper duck and Amber bill on Ink: warm, not DuckDB yellow
